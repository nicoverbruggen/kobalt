About these captures

Captured headlessly through the current desktop application and CREngine, with a fixed 09:00 clock. Every screen has light and dark captures. E-reader profiles also have a light capture with 150% interface text; 150% text is unsupported on the phone profiles because the UI is too dense. Captures use six-inch and seven-inch portrait, phone (720 × 1600), six-inch landscape (1448 × 1072) and phone landscape (1480 × 720) resolutions, at 300 PPI. The phone is a representative 20:9 screen at the reader’s design density, not a named handset. In portrait it has a 60-pixel camera band at the top, which the reader fills with paper, and rounded corners. In landscape the camera side is letterboxed, so the reader is 120 pixels narrower than the screen. These host captures do not validate Android system bars, keyboards or physical touch input. PNGs contain only the screen. The viewer defaults to a representative Kobo Clara frame. Its Clara and Libra frames use the desktop preview proportions and are presentation only. Raw PNGs remain frameless. International fixtures preserve pinned public-domain chapters and Wikisource attribution. CJK vertical variants apply writing-mode styling to the original text. Dictionary replies and service failures use controlled fixtures. Dictionary management uses standalone mode at the same screen sizes. Native operating-system dialogs, hardware light, Bluetooth and firmware sync screens are outside this desktop tour.
