# Settings and controls checks

`desktop.settingsOpeningCover` checks the cover default, saving the logo choice, reopening with the logo and restoring the cover. `ui.openingCoverDisabled` checks that cached and late cover delivery cannot replace the logo or repaint the view, while progress, reopening status and native-reader handoff remain available. The loading gallery includes logo and logo-reopening screens. These checks use headless hosts; physical e-ink refresh remains unverified.

`desktop.settingsRendering` removes the Accurate pin of the test runner. It checks that a book opens as Fast without a saved choice, that choosing Accurate saves `rendering_mode` when Settings closes, and that the next opening is Accurate at the same reading position. It then switches back to Fast and checks the position again. Set `KOBALT_RENDERING_CAPTURES` to an absolute directory to save the six-inch System page with the row and with its choices, at 100% and 150% interface text. `model.appearance-settings` checks the default, invalid values and the order of the override, `KOBALT_CHAPTER_MODE` and the preference. `model.profile-storage` checks the round trip and a preferences file without the key. The gallery includes the choices as Rendering.

`desktop.settingsStandby` checks supported and unsupported hosts, the default state, confirmation, cancellation, unanswered-warning dismissal, persistence after reopening and re-enabling standby. The gallery includes the System settings pages and the standby confirmation.

`desktop.settingsAutomaticOpening` gives the reader a host reader choice. It checks that turning Open books automatically in Kobalt off shows a confirmation, that Cancel and closing Settings keep it on and save nothing, that confirming turns it off and that turning it on applies at once. The gallery includes the confirmation.

`desktop.settingsZenPage` opens Settings in Zen mode with a frontlight and the edge swipes off. It checks that the page has no rail, shows the ZEN caption, Dark mode, Light, About and the hold button, and shows Sync now and Automatic sync only while an account is signed in. Dark mode must apply at once and follow the Display checkbox. About must open with a back cell and without the hold button, and back must return to the page. Light must close Settings and open the Light panel. A press released before the last step must keep Zen mode. A press held through all steps must turn Zen mode off in the draft and show the rail on Display; closing Settings must save it. Settings opened outside Zen mode must show the rail and no Zen page. The tests shorten the half-second step with the button's `reader_hold_step_ms` property instead of waiting.

`desktop.settingsZenMode` turns Zen mode on from the first Display row. It checks the centered groups, the hidden toolbar actions and tabs, and the Chapters and Bookmarks tabs. It checks that the header and footer are blank while the menu is open and return when it closes. It checks that turning Zen mode on and off, a chapter jump and a search jump from the Zen menu each request a full cleanup, and that the cleanup waits while Search covers the page. Leaving Search with Back requests the cleanup as Search closes. Closing Bookmarks with its Zen tab must be a tab-panel dismissal, as closing Chapters is. Opening Chapters and returning from Settings to the Zen menu must each request one screen update. Under Chapters the tabs must keep their place, attach to the view and lose their rounded corners. Paper with the heavy rule along its top must cover the page beside them, from the end of the view to the bottom edge. Chapters and Bookmarks must both end on that rule. It turns the reading stats on and checks the counted page turns, including a page button turn while the menu is open. The panel must stay on after the menu closes and reopens, and the test checks that the choice is saved and cleared. It presses Annotate to turn highlight mode on and off. It then turns Zen mode off with a held press in Settings and checks that the regular toolbar, tabs, Settings width and header and footer return. A second row hides Annotations and Bookmarks and checks that only Chapters and Search remain. A third row hides only Bookmarks and checks that the tab reads Annotations. The gallery captures the Zen menu, its reading stats, the low-battery status line and Chapters opened from it. The low-battery capture checks that the ZEN mark and the battery icon are the same distance from the group's edges.

`desktop.themeChanges` opens the Text and Layout panels, headers and footers, Chapters, Stats and each page of each Settings section in a fresh reader, in light and in dark. It turns dark mode off and on in Settings and checks that each view looks the same as when it was first built in that theme, including views that are first opened after the changes. Light captions and notes use plain ink and dark ones the secondary ink; disabled rows keep plain ink under their dimming in light. `desktop.settingsSectionCaptions` checks in a fresh light reader that the section captions of every Settings page have that plain ink.

`desktop.settingsChoicePopupDismissal` opens the screen refresh and chapter group choices. It checks that a tap on the title or the padding of the card keeps the pop-up open, that a tap on the backdrop closes it and that the row opens it again. `ui.modalClosesOnBackdropOnly` checks the same rule on the shared pop-up card, which the Settings sheets also use. `ui.choiceSheetListFillsCard` checks at 212 and 300 ppi that the list of a choice sheet fills the card inside its border.

`desktop.settingsFeatures` checks the checklist, persistence, re-enabling and dependent defaults. `lifecycle.readingTimeStatsVisibility` uses an injected reading clock and the production checklist to check the elapsed-session message, timer updates, panel switches, persisted visibility and reopening on e-reader and phone viewports. Hidden Stats keeps collecting reading time. `KOBALT_STATS_VISIBILITY_CAPTURE_DIR` saves the initial menu in each preference state and viewport for local screen review. The gallery captures the checklist beside System settings. `desktop.settingsFeatureLists` hides Bookmarks or Annotations on their own and checks the shared list, the toolbar button's label and that the button keeps the toolbar style of the Settings button. `lifecycle.kosyncAccountGating` includes a signed-in account with KOSync disabled and checks that opening, reading and closing make no connection.

`model.text-presets` checks text presets: the examples, cleanup of damaged lists, matching a book's text in any screen density, unique ids and names, the new-book choice, grouping custom presets first, keeping their saved order and restoring the examples after them without dropping the user's presets. `desktop.settingsRestorePresets` cancels and then confirms Restore default presets, and checks that the list changes only when Settings closes and that appearance settings stay. `desktop.reading` drives the Text panel: it opens the preset cards, applies Modern and Kobalt, marks a change, refuses a taken name, saves a new preset, opens All and renames a preset from the edit cell of a tile's actions. `desktop.settingsTextPresetLatestChoice` selects Modern and then Kobalt while the preview is waiting and checks the book uses Kobalt when the panel closes. `desktop.settings` chooses Book default in the Text for new books grid. The gallery captures the cards, a changed preset, the save dialog, All presets, a preset's actions, Settings > Defaults, Text for new books and the Restore default presets confirmation.

Label `settings`. Build `mac-tests`, then run `ctest --preset mac -L settings` from `platforms/desktop/`. `ctest --preset mac -N -L settings` lists its tests.

These checks cover Settings drafts, apply and persistence, appearance previews and their failures, the Light shortcut for dark mode, shared controls, borders, themes and the acknowledgment and license pages. `component.ui` renders the shared controls in pixels in both themes and at 212 and 300 PPI. Its border checks use fractional scales and pixel offsets, because rounding a strip width separately from its boundaries gives uneven borders. It also checks that an option row measured before it is polished uses its style sheet font. On Kobo the application font is Nickel's, so a row measured with it was too short and clipped the dialog around it. The `lifecycle.appearance*` checks drive previews and their failures in the production reader.

Appearance changes use separate contracts. `appearance::Patch` changes only named appearance fields. `appearance::Requested` holds the complete request and cannot carry a reading position. `appearance::Displayed` pairs resolved settings with the completed frame revision. `appearance::SavedInput` preserves absent and null fields when restoring persisted input. The existing `AppearanceEditSession` continues to own preview and apply lifetimes. Model checks cover partial edits and persisted fields; session checks queue edits before a frame completes and verify that displayed and checkpoint settings stay with the old frame until completion. Temporary typography checks verify that checkpoints preserve the original saved appearance.

Dark-mode saves from Settings and from the Light shortcut run on the profile writer thread. These headless checks do not establish physical touch delivery, e-ink refresh quality or device storage latency.

Centered choice and confirmation dialogs use the shared `PopupCard` heading for title and description spacing. The card also joins visible `ListRow`, `RadioList` and `ListHeader` sections: it supplies a top divider after a heading or prose, and reuses the preceding list row or section header's bottom divider. This applies to nested vertical sections and the first visible row on each page. Callers do not add those top dividers themselves. Lists outside a modal retain their container's divider policy. `ui.modalListDividers` checks the rendered boundaries in both themes at 212 and 300 ppi, including paging, plain radio lists and nested section headers. A footer or pager reuses the last visible list row's bottom rule. Side-by-side footer actions share that boundary; stacked actions keep a divider between the buttons. The rendering check covers these seams too, including fixed-height radio lists whose shorter pages leave blank space above the pager. A list supplies that boundary only when its visible rows reach the widget bottom. Existing prose-only footer and dismissal checks remain separate. The gallery includes the KOSync matching chooser, which previously relied on a missing caller divider. Its plain text wraps into balanced lines using the same font measurements as painting. Explicit paragraph breaks stay intact. Long prose keeps ordinary wrapping. The component checks cover orphan-line avoidance, line width, paragraph preservation and text without word spaces. Described option rows reserve extra vertical space when their supporting text determines the row height; rows without descriptions retain their size.

`model.style-sample-cache` checks the key of a kept Styling preview, the separate portrait and landscape sets, the size limit and the one-byte storage of grey pages. `desktop.settingsStylingSamples` holds the engine thread and checks that a preview shown before appears again without it, in the orientation that rendered it. It holds the engine before closing and reopening Settings, and waits for the closed sheet's destruction before looking up controls in the next sheet. `desktop.settingsTextPresetName` uses the Clara screen size to check replacing the selected name with an on-screen key, the native keyboard's deselect-before-key sequence, Backspace, newest-first creation order and unchanged order after use. `desktop.settingsTextPresetLandscape` checks preset access on e-readers in either orientation and its absence on phones in landscape. `desktop.settingsTextPresetGallery` checks that All covers the bottom tabs, offers Save Current Text in the first cell even when the current text matches a preset, and renders each tile of its page once, although showing the grid resizes it and refreshes the reader, that opening All again with the engine thread held shows the same tiles, and that a book opened again prepares the same tiles from storage while the engine thread is held. `model.preview-store` checks that grey and coloured tiles keep their pixels through storage, that missing and damaged files are misses, the storage and memory limits, and that a new store reads what an earlier one wrote. `lifecycle.turnSkipsHiddenControls` checks that a page turn leaves the hidden menu's controls alone and that opening the menu brings them up to date.

Dismissal checks verify that appearance and Styling previews release their source images. Dictionary and footnote panels release their text and layout on dismissal, retain content while their parent is temporarily hidden, and populate again on reopening. These are ownership and behavior checks, not measurements of process RSS or Kobo memory.

Reader teardown checks observe child-panel callbacks through window destruction, with Text and Layout previews ready or still rendering and with Settings open. They reject callbacks after reader teardown starts without relying on an allocator to detect a destroyed member. These checks cover the observed panel subscriptions, not every possible widget callback lifetime.

`component.ui` checks the shared button feedback: its triangle overlaps the target button, it can appear beside a button or below one at the top of the screen, a side offset moves the card without moving the arrow tip, it lets input pass through, and it dismisses automatically.
