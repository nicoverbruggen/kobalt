# Page turns and navigation

Normal page turns include non-linear EPUB sections, including covers marked as non-linear. Settings > Books > Hide non-linear fragments skips other non-linear sections during page turns. It is off by default. Links, Chapters and page-number navigation can still open hidden sections; page turns within a section already open remain available.

Remote page-turn key activity resets Nickel's Bluetooth inactivity timeout while Kobalt is active. Touch page turns do not keep Bluetooth on. Kobalt does not enable Bluetooth or send a periodic heartbeat just because a book is open. When remote activity stops, Nickel retains its normal inactivity shutdown policy.

While a page turn is running, Kobalt accepts one pending turn. Further turns replace its direction. The current page is painted before the pending turn starts. Opening menus, changing settings, sleeping or leaving the reader discards the pending turn. Opening a book and jumping to a passage do not accept pending turns. Navigate can open as soon as a page turn completes.

By default, tap the left third of the page to go back, the right third to go forward and anywhere along the full-height middle strip or the title to open the menus. The Mac e-reader previews use the same tap zones. Swipe left or right to turn pages. For books whose EPUB spine declares right-to-left page progression, the left third advances and the right third goes back; horizontal swipes also reverse. Explicit Next and Previous actions, keyboard arrows and hardware page buttons keep their usual meanings. Hardware page buttons follow Nickel's button mapping and its invert setting until you assign a reading action in Button controls. Page buttons use the topmost open panel and its page arrows. This includes Chapters, Bookmarks, search results, dictionaries, footnotes, settings, font pickers, Stats, header/footer fields, acknowledgments and licenses. Each panel keeps the same wrapping and boundary behavior as its arrows. Definition, footnote and license text stops at its ends. Confirmation dialogs block paging behind them. While Navigate is open, page buttons move its preview by one page. They do not change the reading position until the preview is confirmed. Bluetooth remotes and keyboards turn pages with the arrow keys, like Nickel's reader: right and down go forward, left and up go back. Other remote keys, such as page up and down, volume and media keys, do nothing, as in Nickel's reader.

Paginated lists and text panels also accept horizontal swipes: swipe left for Next and right for Previous. Swipes use the panel's arrows, including their wrapping and end boundaries. Swiping a list row does not select it. Sliders, editable text, scrolling controls and views with their own swipe behavior keep their gestures. A font hold keeps its default-font action. List swipes remain available regardless of the reading page's navigation setting.

Settings > Controls > Page navigation opens a choice of Swipes and taps, Taps only or Swipes only. Swipes and taps is the default. Hosts with page buttons also offer Hardware page turn buttons only. This setting controls page-turn tap zones and horizontal page swipes. Tapping the middle or title still opens the menu in every mode. Controls also has the separate option for vertical brightness and warmth gestures. The Controls tab uses a hand icon and sits directly below Display. The choice is saved for all books. A saved button-only choice falls back to swipes and taps on hosts without page buttons.

Settings > Controls > Hardware buttons opens a centered modal for the host's available physical buttons. The row is hidden when the host has no supported buttons. Tap a button in the device diagram or its action row to choose Previous page, Next page or Do nothing. Do nothing consumes the press without turning a page. Cancel discards the draft; Done saves it for all books without waiting for storage on the reading thread. Android labels its controls Volume up (+) and Volume down (−). Menus and Navigate previews keep the host's usual directions. Keyboards, Bluetooth arrow keys, touch taps and swipes keep their existing behavior. Missing or invalid saved button actions retain the host's native direction, including Nickel's rotation and inversion policy.

Settings > Controls > Tap zones offers four diagrams: Left / right (the default), Reversed, Top / bottom and One-handed. Reversed uses the left third to go forward, the right third to go back and the middle third to open the menu, regardless of the book's page progression. Top / bottom uses the top 40% to go back, the bottom 40% to go forward and the middle 20% to open the menu. One-handed turns forward when you tap outside the marked center menu zone. Swipe to go back or forward. Choosing One-handed enables Swipes and taps. Taps only and Hardware page turn buttons only are unavailable until you choose another tap-zone preset. Invalid saved combinations fall back to Swipes and taps. Left / right follows the book's page progression; Reversed, Top / bottom and One-handed keep their logical back and forward actions. The title and bookmark corner keep their existing actions in every preset.

Non-linear content starts on its own page: spine items marked `linear="no"`, footnote asides and footnote sections. Page turns skip it. A link opens it, and page turns then stay in it until it ends. Page numbers and progress still count its pages. At the last page of the linear text, a forward turn asks whether to mark the book finished.

Chapters uses the EPUB's table of contents on every platform. Counting and chapter navigation do not require Nickel's chapter layouts or word-count parser.

Nested chapter groups have a plus or minus in a separate cell on the left. Tap that cell to expand or collapse the group. Tap the title to open the chapter. A heading without a destination can still expand its children. Settings > Controls > Chapter List > Collapsible chapters selects Current section, All chapters or Off. Off shows the full indented list without disclosure buttons. Current section is the default and opens the current chapter's ancestors while keeping other groups collapsed. All chapters opens every group and keeps the disclosure buttons available. Expansion stays while paging or resizing the panel and resets when Chapters opens again. Chapter numbers and page numbers keep their original values. Collapsing groups does not change reading progress or Stats.

## Links and footnotes

- Standard Ebooks Kobo link wrappers use their publisher border without a second default underline. Ordinary links retain their default underline. Publisher link colors and decorations remain available.

- Hold link text for 500 ms to select its word and open the shared selection popover. Follow link performs the link action directly without another action menu. By default, taps open recognised footnotes in a panel. Taps on other links still turn pages or open the reading menu. Follow link opens a recognised footnote or endnote in a sheet inside the reading area. Kobalt uses explicit note markup when available. Without it, short numbered or lettered references, Roman numerals and superscript or subscript references can identify a note. Links can include images, including image-only references inside superscript or subscript markup. Inferred notes must follow the reference, stay within 10,000 characters and have readable text. Chapter destinations and links that fill a paragraph stay ordinary links. The sheet's last row, Show in book, jumps to the note in the book; a small uppercase caption below it names the chapter that holds the note, without a trailing colon or full stop. The reading status strip draws a divider whenever a panel meets its lower edge. A note without a note container continues in the paragraphs after its first one, up to the next element with an ID or legacy anchor name, a heading, a new section or a forced page break. Other internal links jump after you choose Follow link. Return to passage appears as a footer row only while the reading menu is open. Closing the menu keeps the saved passage. The row shows an undo icon, Return to page followed by the original page number, and a small uppercase caption naming the original chapter. Its close button discards the return position.
- For a web link, Kobalt opens a centered confirmation modal with the full URL and asks whether to open it in the browser. Choose Open to open an HTTP or HTTPS link in Kobo's built-in browser or the Android or desktop browser. Choose Cancel to keep reading. If the browser is unavailable, the URL stays visible and Open is disabled.

Settings > Controls > Links includes Tappable links with three choices: Footnotes only, Footnotes and links, and Always require long press. Footnotes only is the default. Always require long press keeps page and menu actions on link taps. Footnotes only uses the same note detection as Follow link. Footnotes and links also includes passage, website and unavailable links. The choice applies to all books and survives a restart. Links keep their existing styling in both themes. Kobalt's default link style is underlined; a publisher can override it. Enabling direct taps does not change pagination.

Tap a footnote reference or within its circular target to open its panel. Only references narrower than their height get circular targets; wide footnote links keep their text-sized targets. Each circle is centered on its reference and has the same height as the link highlight rectangle. This gives narrow references extra space on either side. Exact link text takes priority; overlapping footnote targets choose the nearest reference rectangle. Other links keep their text-sized targets for passage navigation or website confirmation. Direct taps take priority over page tap zones and the bookmark corner, including when page-turn taps are disabled. Taps outside these targets keep the configured page, menu and bookmark actions. Direct taps are inactive while reading controls are open or annotation mode is enabled. Long presses keep word selection, dictionary lookup and Follow link. Highlight links remains available in all three modes.

Kobalt keeps the book’s link styling. When reading controls are open, pages containing links show a compact Highlight links button with the link count above the scrubber or bottom tabs. Tap the button to reveal the links: each link gets a gray background and an outline. Narrow footnote and endnote references use circles instead of rectangles. Wide note links keep rectangular highlights. Overlapping circles are clipped where the neighboring link takes priority, using the same nearest-rectangle rule as taps. Pressing a revealed link slightly changes its gray highlight until release. Highlight links accepts taps only: holding does not select text, and swipes and annotation shortcuts are inactive. Releasing a stationary press follows the link; dragging cancels it. Tap a link to follow it; the reading controls stay closed. Reveal mode hides the normal toolbars so they cannot cover links. A label replaces the header strip, or the footer strip when the header is too small, in that strip’s face and size: “Tap a highlighted link to follow it” on the left and “Tap elsewhere to exit” on the right. Tap anywhere except a link, the label included, to leave reveal mode. You return to the reading page with the toolbars closed, after opening highlight mode from the controls. The dismissal tap does not also open the controls or turn a page. In ordinary reading, a long press on link text selects only the word under your finger and opens its selection popover. Dictionary, Follow link and Clear selection share one connected row. Dictionary opens only when chosen. The menu has no annotation actions. Enable highlight mode in the reading menu before selecting text to annotate. Follow link is available in the regular selection popover and is omitted from the marking popover.

## Search

- Search uses Kobalt's keyboard on Kobo and Mac and the system keyboard on Android. It returns up to 500 results. The Kobalt keyboard follows the reader's light or dark mode. It takes a third of the screen height, but at least four rows of keys as tall as their labels, so a short screen keeps keys that can be tapped. On Android, Search keeps the capitalization you type. Submitting a query closes the keyboard and stops editing the query. Tap the query to edit it again. If a layout change interrupts a search, Kobalt restarts it after the new layout is ready and replaces its partial results. Closing Search or submitting another query cancels the previous search. It ignores case, and it matches ligatures such as "ﬁ", non-breaking hyphens and zero-width characters as typed. Selecting a result highlights the match until the displayed page changes. Page turns and typesetting previews clear this temporary highlight. Each result shows its page and chapter, and an excerpt in the font the book is displayed in. With Publisher fonts, the excerpt uses the book's own face when it is a bundled, built-in or sideloaded font, and Libron otherwise.
