# Image viewer

Long-press a large illustration or the cover on a reading page to open the image viewer. Small ornaments and narrow wordmarks keep their reading gestures. Eligibility uses the displayed image area, so low-resolution illustrations can still open. Image-only footnote references keep their note behavior. When a page contains exactly one eligible image and no body text, opening the reading controls shows a floating Zoom image button. It opens the same viewer. Its close button dismisses the suggestion until the reading position changes. Zoom image and Return to page appear side by side when both full labels and their close buttons fit. If they do not fit, Return to page takes priority. Long-pressing the image still opens the viewer.

An image opens at its remembered view, or fitted to the available space if no view is saved. Use Plus and Minus or pinch to zoom. Drag to pan. Double-tap to switch between Fit and a two-times enlargement. The image-in-corners button on the left fits the whole image and resets its remembered view. Minus and Plus sit together on the right. Each image remembers its zoom and center point within the book, including after closing and reopening the book. An image without a saved view starts fitted. The saved center adapts to the viewport and stays within the image edges. Zoom stops at eight times the fitted size. Minus is disabled at Fit, and Plus is disabled at maximum zoom. Images keep their original colours in both themes. Close, Escape or Back returns to the same reading position. Page buttons do not turn pages while the viewer is open. Sleeping or hiding the reader closes the viewer.

Pan, pinch and mouse-wheel previews update at most once every 100 milliseconds on all current platforms. Releasing a gesture shows the latest position immediately and requests a quality refresh. Mouse-wheel input settles after the same interval without input.

On desktop, the mouse wheel also zooms. This prototype uses the image's source resolution, including for SVG rendering. Physical touch and e-ink refresh remain unverified.
